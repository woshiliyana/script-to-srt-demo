You have a finished script and a voiceover. Here's how to turn them into an SRT subtitle file, without retyping or re-transcribing a single word.

Open Script to Subtitles on TimedSubs, and paste your script, or upload it as a text file.

Then upload the matching voiceover. MP3, WAV, M4A and FLAC all work.

TimedSubs listens to the audio and lines up each line of your script with the moment it's spoken. Your wording stays exactly as you wrote it.

When it's ready, check the timeline. If part of the audio doesn't match the script, that spot is flagged, so you know where to look.

Download SRT or VTT, and drop it into YouTube, CapCut, or Premiere.
